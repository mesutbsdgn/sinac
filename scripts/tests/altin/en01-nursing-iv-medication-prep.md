# IV medication preparation: checklist

## 1. The five rights

1. Right patient
2. Right drug
3. Right dose
4. Right route
5. Right time

Check the order, the label and the wristband before every preparation.

## 2. Sample order table

| Drug | Dose | Route | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Furosemide | 40 mg | IV | once daily |
| Morphine sulfate | 5.0 mg | IV slow push | every 4 hours as needed |
| Ondansetron | 4 mg | IV | every 8 hours |

## 3. Preparation steps

- Wash hands and prepare the medication on a clean surface.
- Wipe the ampoule neck with an alcohol swab before opening.
- Label the syringe: drug name, dose, time.
- Insulin regular 10 U SC is prepared separately and double-checked by a second nurse.

## 4. Self-check

1. List the five rights without looking.
2. Why must an unlabeled syringe never be used?
3. What do you do if an opioid order is unclear?
