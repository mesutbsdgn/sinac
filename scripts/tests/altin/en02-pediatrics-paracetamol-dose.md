# Paediatric paracetamol: weight-based dosing

## 1. Key facts

Paracetamol is dosed by weight in children. The calculation has two steps: total mg, then mL.

- Dose: 15 mg/kg per dose, up to every 6 hours (source: national paediatric formulary, 2024)
- Syrup strength: 120 mg/5 mL
- Maximum single dose: 1,000 mg; the calculated dose must not exceed this.

## 2. Worked examples

Example 1: 8 kg infant, 8 kg × 15 mg/kg = 120 mg per dose. That is 5 mL of 120 mg/5 mL syrup.

Example 2: 15 kg × 15 mg/kg = 325 mg per dose.

Example 3: 24 kg child, 24 kg × 15 mg/kg = 360 mg per dose, which is 15 mL.

## 3. Common error

Mixing up the daily total with the single dose. Always check whether the order says mg/kg/day or mg/kg/dose.
