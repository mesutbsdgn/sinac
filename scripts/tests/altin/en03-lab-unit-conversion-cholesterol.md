# Laboratory unit conversion

## 1. Why convert?

Some countries report glucose and cholesterol in mg/dL and others in mmol/L. The factor depends on the analyte.

## 2. Factors

| Analyte | mg/dL to mmol/L |
|---|---|
| Glucose | × 0.0555 |
| Total cholesterol | × 0.0259 |
| Triglycerides | × 0.0113 |
| Creatinine | divide by 11.3 |

## 3. Examples

- Triglycerides 150 mg/dL ≈ 1.7 mmol/L.
- Total cholesterol 200 mg/dL = 0.52 mmol/L.
- Glucose 126 mg/dL ≈ 7.0 mmol/L, the fasting diabetes threshold.
- Creatinine 1.0 mg/dL ≈ 88 µmol/L.

## 4. Reference range

Fasting glucose between 70 and 100 mg/dL is usually considered normal.
