# Dispensing and counselling: short-course benzodiazepine

## 1. Checks before dispensing

1. Prescription date, prescriber signature and quantity.
2. Drug, strength and form read aloud against the label.
3. Allergy and interaction screen.

## 2. Example prescription lines

- Rx: lorazepam .5 mg PO twice daily as needed, 14 tablets
- Rx: omeprazole 20 mg PO once daily before breakfast, 28 capsules

## 3. Counselling points

- May cause drowsiness; avoid driving until the effect is known.
- Do not combine with alcohol.
- Short courses lower the risk of dependence.

## 4. Label wording

Write "once daily", not "q.d." and avoid trailing zeros. A tablet labelled 0.25 mg is clearly distinct from 2.5 mg.
