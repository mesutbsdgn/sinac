# Verifying weight-based doses

## 1. Principle

A nurse recalculates every weight-based dose independently before administration. This note uses vancomycin at 15 mg/kg per dose as a teaching example.

## 2. Order verification table

| Weight | Ordered dose rate | Calculated dose |
|---|---|---|
| 60 kg | 15 mg/kg | 900 mg |
| 70 kg | 15 mg/kg | **1,150 mg** |
| 80 kg | 15 mg/kg | 1,200 mg |

## 3. Steps

- Weigh the patient; do not use an old chart weight.
- Multiply weight by the rate, then compare with the pharmacy label.
- If the numbers differ, stop and call the pharmacist.

## 4. Documentation

Record the weight, the rate, the calculated dose and the time of the check.

## 5. Self-check

1. Calculate the dose for a 55 kg patient.
2. What do you do if your result differs from the label?
