# Shift handover with SBAR

## 1. What is SBAR?

Situation, background, assessment, recommendation: a four-part structure for short, complete handover.

## 2. Example handover

**Situation:** Bed 12, man with a COPD exacerbation. Handover note for bed 12 (MRN 6620419).

**Background:** Improved after yesterday evening's nebuliser; two episodes of breathlessness overnight.

**Assessment:** SpO2 92%, respiratory rate 22/min, afebrile.

**Recommendation:** Keep nebuliser times on schedule; call the physician if SpO2 falls below 90%.

## 3. Good handover

- Takes under two minutes.
- Uses measured values instead of vague words such as "a bit high".
- States what is to be done and when.
