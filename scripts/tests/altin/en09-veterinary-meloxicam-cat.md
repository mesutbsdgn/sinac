# Meloxicam in cats: single-dose calculation

## 1. Background

Meloxicam is an NSAID. In cats it is used with caution and only short term; kidney function is checked first.

## 2. Dose

- Single injectable dose: 0.05 mg/kg
- Never repeat without veterinary instruction.

## 3. Worked example

Case: 4 kg domestic cat, postoperative pain.
4 kg × 0.05 mg/kg = 0.2 mg.

## 4. Cautions

- Dehydration or kidney disease is a reason to avoid it.
- Do not combine with another NSAID or a corticosteroid.
- Dogs have a different dose table.
