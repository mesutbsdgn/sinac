# Estimating energy and protein needs

## 1. Goal

Estimate daily energy and protein needs for an adult patient.

## 2. Body mass index

BMI = weight (kg) / height (m) squared.

Example: 70 kg and 1.75 m gives BMI = 70 / (1.75)^2 = 22.9 kg/m².

## 3. Protein

A common planning figure for a healthy adult is 1.0 g protein per kg body weight per day. Patients with higher needs, such as those recovering from surgery, require individual assessment.

## 4. Energy

A simple screening estimate is 25 to 30 kcal per kg per day. Prediction equations such as Harris-Benedict give a more detailed estimate.

## 5. Notes

- Weigh the patient at a consistent time of day.
- Record the method used; estimates differ between equations.

## 6. Self-check

2. Why is a single protein figure not enough for every patient?
