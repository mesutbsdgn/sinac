# Glucose result and group comparison

## 1. Clinical vignette

Patient: 54-year-old man, routine check. Fasting glucose is 90 mg/dL.

## 2. Unit conversion

Multiply by 0.0555: glucose 90 mg/dL ≈ 5.0 mmol/L. A result of 5.0 mmol/L is within the usual fasting range.

Check the converted number against the original: the mmol/L figure should be about 18 times smaller.

## 3. A group comparison

A trial compared fasting glucose after diet advice with no advice. Mean difference 0.6 mmol/L, p < 0.001.

## 4. Reading the p-value

- p < 0.001 means the data would be very unlikely if there were no true difference.
- It does not give the size or clinical importance of the difference.
