# Anaphylaxis: first response

## 1. Recognition

Sudden skin signs together with breathing or circulation problems suggest anaphylaxis.

## 2. First step

- Lay the patient flat and raise the legs.
- Epinephrine 0.5 mg IM into the outer mid-thigh.
- Give oxygen and obtain IV access.
- Repeat the dose after 5 to 15 minutes if there is no clear response.

## 3. Children

Children receive 0.01 mg/kg IM, up to 0.5 mg. Example: 20 kg × 0.01 mg/kg = 0.2 mg.

## 4. Writing doses safely

Do not write 5.0 mg; write 5 mg. Do not write .5 mg; write 0.5 mg. These rules prevent tenfold errors.

## 5. Second line

Antihistamine and steroid help only as additions; they do not replace epinephrine.
