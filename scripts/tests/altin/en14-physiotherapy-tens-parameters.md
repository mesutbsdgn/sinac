# Electrotherapy parameters: TENS and ultrasound

## 1. TENS

Conventional TENS uses high frequency and low intensity.

- Frequency: 100 Hz
- Pulse width: 150 µs
- Intensity: below the motor threshold

## 2. Therapeutic ultrasound

Typical continuous ultrasound for a superficial tendon: frequency 3 MHz, intensity 1.0 W/cm², treatment time 5 minutes, using a moving head.

## 3. Case example

A 45-year-old with lateral elbow pain scored 6 out of 10 at rest. After two weeks the range of motion rose from 90° to 120°.

## 4. Safety

- Check skin sensation before every session.
- Avoid pacemaker patients and over the abdomen of pregnant patients.
- Document the settings used in every session.
