# French basics for Turkish-speaking learners

## 1. Vocabulary

| French | Pronunciation | English | Turkish |
|---|---|---|---|
| la classe | la klas | class | sınıf |
| la station | la stasyon | stop, station | durak |
| la nation | la nasyon | nation | ulus |
| l'école | lekol | school | okul |
| le livre | lö livr | book | kitap |

## 2. Words that look like English

"classe", "station" and "nation" look like English words. The meaning is shared, but the French spelling and pronunciation differ.

Careful: "actuellement" means "currently", not "actually".

## 3. Example sentences

- La classe commence à neuf heures. (The class starts at nine.)
- La station est près de l'école. (The station is near the school.)
