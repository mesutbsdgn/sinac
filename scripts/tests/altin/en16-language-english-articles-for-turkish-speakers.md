# English articles for Turkish speakers

## 1. The problem

Turkish has no article, so learners often drop a, an or the.

## 2. Rules

- Use a before a consonant sound: a station.
- Use an before a vowel sound: an hour.
- Use the for something specific or already mentioned.

## 3. Examples

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| I go to station. | I go to the station. |
| She is teacher. | She is a teacher. |
| We belong to same nation. | We belong to the same nation. |

## 4. Turkish helper

Turkish "bir" is close to "a" but is used less often than English "a".

## 5. Self-check

1. Fill in: She is ___ honest person.
2. Fix: He joined class last year.
3. Write two sentences about your city with the.
