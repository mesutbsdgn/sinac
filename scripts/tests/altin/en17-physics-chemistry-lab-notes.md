# Lab notes: electricity and solutions

## 1. Electricity lab

A battery supplies 5.0 V across a resistor and 2.0 A flows. R = 5.0 V / 2.0 A = 2.5 Ω.

Power: P = 5.0 V × 2.0 A = 10 W.

## 2. Chemistry lab: making a solution

1. Weigh 0.50 g of sodium chloride.
2. Dissolve it in about 50 mL of water in a 100 mL volumetric flask.
3. Fill to the mark; a 25.0 mL pipette transfers a sample.

0.50 g of NaCl is about 0.0085 mol (molar mass 58.5 g/mol); in 0.100 L that gives about 0.085 mol/L.

## 3. Significant figures

Trailing zeros after the decimal point show measurement precision in a lab report.

## 4. Safety

Wear goggles and a coat. Wipe spills immediately.
