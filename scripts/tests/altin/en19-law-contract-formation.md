# Contract law: formation and limits

## 1. Formation

A contract is formed by matching declarations of intent. In the Turkish Code of Obligations (Law No. 6098) this is in Article 1.

## 2. Void contracts

A contract that conflicts with mandatory provisions or morality is void (Article 27).

## 3. Default

A debtor in default after a creditor's formal notice owes default interest (Article 117).

## 4. Limitation

The general limitation period is ten years (Article 146).

## 5. Tort

A person who causes damage by a wrongful and faulty act must compensate it (Article 49).
