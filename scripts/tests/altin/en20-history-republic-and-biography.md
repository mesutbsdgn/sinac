# Early republic: a timeline

## 1. Timeline

- 29 October 1923: the republic was proclaimed.
- 1930: the public health law was adopted.
- 1453: the conquest of Istanbul, shown here only as a reference date.

## 2. Fictional biography

Margaret Holloway (born 04/02/1899, died 1971) is a fictional public health nurse who stands for health workers of the period.

## 3. Key terms

- Dispensary
- Control of infectious disease
- Maternal and child health

## 4. Cause and effect

Population loss after war speeded up central planning of health services.
