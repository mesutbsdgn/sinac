# Completing the Square: Worked Notes

## The idea

Every quadratic can be rewritten as a perfect square plus a constant.

## Example

x² + 6x + 5 = (x + 3)² − 4

Check: (x + 3)² − 4 = x² + 6x + 9 − 4 = x² + 6x + 5.

## Solving

(x + 3)² = 4 gives x + 3 = ±2, so x = −1 or x = −5.

## Common slips

- Forgetting to subtract b²/4 after adding it.
- Losing the ± when taking a square root.
- Mixing up the sign of the vertex x-coordinate: it is −b/2.

## Self-check

1. Write x² − 8x + 11 in the form (x + p)² + q.
2. Solve x² − 8x + 11 = 0.
