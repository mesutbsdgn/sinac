# Converting Lab Units at the Bedside

Ward charts mix SI and conventional units. Convert before you compare a result with a reference range.

## Glucose

- Conversion: mg/dL ÷ 18 = mmol/L.
- A glucose of 126 mg/dL is written as 5.4 mmol/L in the chart.
- Reference fasting range: 4 to 6 mmol/L (72 to 108 mg/dL).

## Creatinine

- Conversion: mg/dL × 88.4 = µmol/L.
- Creatinine 1.2 mg/dL equals about 106 µmol/L.
- A rise from 88 to 133 µmol/L is clinically meaningful.

## Lipids

- Total cholesterol: mg/dL ÷ 38.67 = mmol/L.
- Total cholesterol 200 mg/dL ≈ 5.2 mmol/L.
- Triglycerides: mg/dL ÷ 88.5 = mmol/L.
- Triglycerides 150 mg/dL ≈ 1.7 mmol/L.

## Practice points

- Always write the unit next to the number.
- Recheck any value that falls far outside the local reference range.
- Ask the laboratory which unit it reports when a number looks implausible.
