# Canine Respiratory Case: Dosing Sheet

## Case frame

- Signalment: 4-year-old neutered male beagle, 12 kg.
- Presenting sign: moist cough for three days, rectal temperature 39.1 °C.
- No vomiting, appetite reduced but water intake normal.

## Drug plan

- Amoxicillin-clavulanate: 12.5 mg/kg twice daily by mouth.
- Give enrofloxacin at 5.0 mg/kg once daily for five days.
- Supportive care: nebulisation twice daily, short walks only.

## Arithmetic check

- Amoxicillin dose per administration: 12.5 × 12 = 150 mg.
- Enrofloxacin dose per administration: 5 × 12 = 60 mg.
- Total daily enrofloxacin: 60 mg once daily.

## Recording

- Write weight, drug, dose in mg/kg, and total mg in the treatment sheet.
- Note the start date and the planned review after 48 hours.
- Recheck renal function before extending any course beyond seven days.
