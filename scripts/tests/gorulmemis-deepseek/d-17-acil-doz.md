# Emergency Medicine: Weight-Based Dosing Drills

## 1. Why weight-based dosing matters
Doses in emergency care are often expressed per kilogram. Always confirm the patient's measured weight, not an estimate.

## 2. Worked examples
- Fentanyl 1 mcg/kg IV for a 70 kg adult gives 70 mcg.
- Ketamine 1.5 mg/kg IM for an 80 kg adult gives 120 mg.
- Naloxone infusion 0.05 mg/kg/h for a 70 kg adult gives 0.35 mg/h.
- Propofol 2 mg/kg IV for a 60 kg adult gives 120 mg.

## 3. Recheck before pushing
- Recalculate independently when a dose looks unusual.
- Confirm units: mcg versus mg is a common error.

## 4. Documentation
Record weight, dose, route and time for every push-dose medication.
