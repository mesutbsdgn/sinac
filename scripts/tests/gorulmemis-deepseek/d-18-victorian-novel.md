# The Victorian Novel: Narration and Social Scope

## 1. The form
The Victorian novel is typically serialised, multiplot, and openly addressed to a broad readership. Narrators often speak directly to the reader.

## 2. Case study: George Eliot
Mary Ann Evans published under a male pen name. She was born on 22 November 1819 in Nuneaton, Warwickshire. Her novels move from provincial comedy to studies of inheritance and reform.

- *The Mill on the Floss* centres on sibling loyalty and constrained choice.
- *Middlemarch* braids four marriages against the backdrop of political change.

## 3. Features to look for
- An omniscient narrator who comments and judges.
- Detailed domestic settings used to carry social argument.
- Chapter endings that withhold resolution to sustain serial interest.

## 4. Discussion prompt
Pick one narrator aside and explain what it asks the reader to believe about a character.
