# Sepsis: First-Hour Recognition and Action

## 1. Case context
Mr. Daniel Okafor, MRN 4471902, presented with fever, confusion and a systolic pressure of 88 mmHg. He is the patient used throughout this note.

## 2. Screening
- Suspect sepsis when infection is paired with new organ dysfunction.
- Track respiratory rate, mental status and urine output.
- Repeat lactate if the first value is raised.

## 3. First hour
- Draw blood cultures before antibiotics when this does not delay treatment.
- Give broad-spectrum antibiotics within one hour.
- Start 30 mL/kg crystalloid for hypotension or lactate of 4 mmol/L or more.

## 4. Reassessment
Recheck pressure, perfusion and urine output after the fluid bolus. Escalate to vasopressors if pressure stays low.
