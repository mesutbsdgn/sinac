# Acute Coronary Syndrome and Lipid Profile Interpretation

## 1. Clinical Presentation and Initial Diagnosis
Acute coronary syndrome (ACS) encompasses unstable angina, NSTEMI, and STEMI. Physicians evaluate chest pain characteristics, serial electrocardiograms, and high-sensitivity troponin levels to guide urgent therapy and risk stratification.

## 2. Lipid Panel Unit Conversions
International guidelines report lipid concentrations in either conventional units (mg/dL) or SI units (mmol/L):
- **Total cholesterol:** divide mg/dL by 38.67 to obtain mmol/L.
- **Triglycerides:** divide mg/dL by 88.5 to obtain mmol/L.

## 4. Secondary Prevention Principles
Following stabilization, management includes dual antiplatelet therapy, high-intensity statin medication, blood pressure control, and structured cardiac rehabilitation.
