# Post-ACL Physiotherapy Rehabilitation Protocol

## 1. Rehabilitation Phases and Goals
Rehabilitation after anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) reconstruction follows criterion-based progression rather than fixed calendar dates alone. Early priorities include effusion control, full knee extension range of motion, and quadriceps activation.

## 2. FITT Exercise Prescription Example (Weeks 2–6)
- **Frequency:** 4 sessions per week.
- **Intensity:** Low-to-moderate load (RPE 3–4/10), pain kept at or below 2/10 on the numeric rating scale.
- **Time:** 3 sets × 12 repetitions for closed-chain mini-squats and heel slides.
- **Type:** Isometric quadriceps sets, stationary cycling, and single-leg balance proprioception drills.
- **Stop criteria:** Acute joint swelling, sharp mechanical locking, or sudden instability.

## 3. Medical Screening Context in Older Physiotherapy Clients
Physiotherapists frequently review routine laboratory summaries during pre-exercise screening:
- In older male clients, a routine prostate screening threshold is PSA 4.0 ng/mL, which requires physician follow-up if exceeded.
- Fasting blood glucose of 90 mg/dL (5.0 mmol/L) indicates normoglycemia prior to aerobic conditioning.
- Clients taking bone-health supplements commonly report daily intake of 1,000 mg calcium alongside vitamin D as advised by their physician.

## Self-Check
1. What are the four components of the FITT exercise prescription framework?
2. Why is full passive knee extension prioritized in early post-ACL physiotherapy?

### Answers
1. Frequency, Intensity, Time, and Type.
2. Persistent extension deficit alters gait mechanics and increases anterior knee pain risk.
