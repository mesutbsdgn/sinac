# Paediatric analgesia: worked examples

Weight-based dosing is the rule in paediatrics. Always confirm the weight on the day of prescribing.

## Paracetamol

A 15 kg toddler receives paracetamol 15 mg/kg every 6 hours. Each dose is 225 mg.

## Ibuprofen

A 24 kg child is prescribed ibuprofen 10 mg/kg every 8 hours; each dose is therefore 420 mg.
Ibuprofen is avoided in dehydration and in children with renal impairment.
