# Heparin infusion protocol: nursing notes

Unfractionated heparin is a high-alert medicine. Two nurses check the calculation independently.

## Standard bag

The pharmacy supplies 25,000 units in 250 mL, which is 100 units/mL.

## Worked example

The patient weighs 80 kg. The protocol starts the infusion at 18 units/kg/h, so the hourly dose is 1,620 units/h.
The bolus is 80 units/kg, which gives 6,400 units.

## Monitoring

Check the aPTT 6 hours after any rate change. Report bleeding immediately.
