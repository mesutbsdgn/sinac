# Two pioneers: Curie and Nightingale

## Marie Curie

Marie Curie won Nobel Prizes in physics and chemistry. She died in 1934, aged 66, of aplastic anaemia,
probably caused by long exposure to radiation. Her notebooks are still radioactive.

## Florence Nightingale

In 1854 Florence Nightingale, a 34-year-old nurse from a wealthy family, led a team of nurses to Scutari
during the Crimean War. Her statistical diagrams persuaded Parliament to reform army hospitals.

## Why they matter

Both women turned careful record keeping into evidence, and evidence into reform.
