# Reading a lipid and renal panel

Laboratories report in conventional units in some countries and SI units in others. You must be able to move between them.

## Case panel

The patient is a middle-aged man seen for a routine check.

- Her LDL cholesterol was 160 mg/dL (4.1 mmol/L) at the previous visit.
- Fasting triglycerides were 300 mg/dL (7.8 mmol/L).
- Serum creatinine was 1.5 mg/dL, or about 133 µmol/L.

## Rule of thumb

Divide cholesterol in mg/dL by about 39 and triglycerides by about 89 to get mmol/L.
