# Significant figures in the physics lab

## Reading instruments

A newton meter reads a force of 5.0 N. The trailing zero matters: it tells the reader the instrument resolves tenths.

- A trolley of mass 2.0 kg accelerates at 1.5 m/s².
- The ruler shows a length of .5 m, which should be written 0.5 m.
- We dissolved 0.50 g of salt in 100 mL of water for the density experiment.

## Rule

Keep as many figures as the least precise measurement allows.

## Check yourself

1. How many significant figures are in 5.0 N?
2. What force acts on the trolley?
3. Why is 0.50 g different from 0.5 g?
