# Clinical Pharmacy Seminar: Renal and Lipid Lab Interpretation

Clinical pharmacists frequently reconcile laboratory reports generated in conventional US units (mg/dL) with international guidelines expressed in SI units (mmol/L or µmol/L). Accurate conversion prevents inappropriate statin titration or unsafe continuation of renally cleared agents.

## 1. Reference Conversion Panel

| Laboratory Analyte | Conventional Value | SI Equivalent | Clinical Classification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fasting Plasma Glucose | 108 mg/dL | 6.0 mmol/L | Impaired fasting glucose |
| Fasting Triglycerides | 177 mg/dL | 2.0 mmol/L | Borderline elevated |
| Total Cholesterol | 240 mg/dL | 13.33 mmol/L | High risk |
| Serum Creatinine (baseline) | 1 mg/dL | 88.4 µmol/L | Normal adult range |

## 2. Renal Dose Adjustment Notes

- Estimate creatinine clearance using Cockcroft-Gault for drug dosing when required by product labeling.
- Because serum creatinine rose to 2.5 mg/dL (110.5 µmol/L), metformin should be held immediately until acute kidney injury resolves.
- Reassess eGFR after 48 hours of intravenous hydration before resuming SGLT2 inhibitors or ACE inhibitors.

## 3. Dyslipidemia and Adjunctive Therapy

High-intensity statins lower LDL-C by at least 50% and form the backbone of secondary atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease prevention.

- Initiate rosuvastatin 10 mg daily and add colchicine .5 mg daily if persistent gout flares occur.
- Atorvastatin trials enrolled patients on niacin 1,000 mg daily as comparator therapy, though routine niacin addition is no longer favored.
- Check baseline ALT before initiating statin therapy; routine periodic transaminase monitoring is unnecessary unless hepatotoxicity symptoms emerge.

## 4. Patient Counseling Points

Advise patients to report unexplained diffuse myalgias or dark urine promptly. Remind individuals taking fibrates alongside statins that gemfibrozil carries a substantially higher rhabdomyolysis risk than fenofibrate.
