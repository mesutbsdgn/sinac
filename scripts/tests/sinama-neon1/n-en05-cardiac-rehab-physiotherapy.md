# Cardiopulmonary Physiotherapy: Phase II Cardiac Rehabilitation

Outpatient Phase II cardiac rehabilitation begins 2 to 6 weeks after myocardial infarction, percutaneous coronary intervention, or coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG). Physical therapists combine telemetry monitoring with structured aerobic progression to restore functional endurance safely.

## 1. Initial Exercise Prescription

- Case example: Mr. K., a 59-year-old post-CABG patient, achieved a resting heart rate of 68 bpm and stable sternal healing at week 4.
- Begin treadmill ambulation at 3.0 km/h and 2.0% grade (approximately 3.0 METs), advancing stride length by 5.0 cm as tolerated.
- Target an exercise intensity of 40% to 60% heart rate reserve (Karvonen method) or Borg Rating of Perceived Exertion (RPE) 11–13 on the 6–20 scale.
- Include a 5-minute warm-up and a 5-minute cool-down to prevent post-exercise venous pooling and hypotension.

## 2. Metabolic and Hemodynamic Safety Thresholds

| Parameter | Safe to Initiate | Defer or Terminate Session |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Resting blood pressure | < 180/100 mmHg | Systolic drop > 10 mmHg with workload |
| Resting heart rate | 50–100 bpm | New-onset atrial fibrillation or VT |
| Pre-exercise glucose | 90–252 mg/dL (5.0–14.0 mmol/L) | < 72 mg/dL (4.0 mmol/L) or ketonuria |
| Oxygen saturation (SpO₂) | ≥ 92% on room air | Drop below 88% during exertion |

- Defer aerobic training if pre-exercise capillary glucose exceeds 252 mg/dL (14.0 mmol/L) with ketonuria.
- For patients taking beta-blockers, rely heavily on Borg RPE and the talk test because heart rate response is blunted.

## 3. Sternal Precautions After Median Sternotomy

Avoid asymmetric upper-extremity loading, forceful pushing off armrests, and lifting objects heavier than 4.5 kg during the first 6 to 8 postoperative weeks. Gentle shoulder flexion and diaphragmatic breathing exercises may start early if pain remains below 3/10.
