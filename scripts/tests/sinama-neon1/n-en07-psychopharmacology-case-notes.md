# Clinical Psychology Seminar: Psychotherapy and Mood Stabilizer Consultation

Psychologists working in integrated behavioral health clinics routinely coordinate care with prescribing psychiatrists while delivering interpersonal and social rhythm therapy (IPSRT) or cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT). Australian psychiatrist John Cade (1912–1980) published his landmark lithium study at age 37 in 1949, transforming longitudinal mood stabilization.

## 1. Psychotherapy Role in Adherence and Early Warning Tracking

- Help clients construct a daily mood and sleep-wake chart to identify prodromal hypomanic shifts early.
- Use motivational interviewing to explore ambivalence toward long-term pharmacotherapy without taking a coercive stance.
- Document side-effect complaints behaviorally and share structured consultation notes with the prescribing clinician after obtaining written release of information.

## 3. Interdisciplinary Reference Parameters

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| Suicide risk screening | Active ideation or preparatory behavior | Same-day crisis safety plan |
