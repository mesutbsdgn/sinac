# Contract Law Outline: Breach Remedies and Damages

Common law contract remedies prioritize compensation over punishment. Courts ordinarily grant monetary damages rather than compelling performance unless the subject matter is unique, such as real property or rare artwork.

## 1. Core Monetary Interests

- Expectation damages aim to place the injured pаrty in the financial position full performance would have produced.
- Reliance damages reimburse out-of-pocket expenses incurred in reasonable anticipation of performance when expectation profits are too speculative to prove.
- Restitution prevents unjust enrichment by returning the measurable benefit conferred upon the breaching party.

## 2. Worked Commercial Hypotheticals

Hypothetical: Buyer A contracts with Supplier B for 500 units at a fixed price. If Seller breaches a contract worth 1,000 USD and cover costs 1,350 USD, direct damages equal 350 USD plus any foreseeable incidental shipping expenses.

| Remedy Interest | Primary Goal | Typical Measure | Availability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Expectation | Benefit of the bargain | Loss in value + other loss − cost avoided | Default remedy |
| Reliance | Status quo ante | Reasonable expenditures prior to breach | When expectation uncertain |
| Restitution | Disgorgement of gain | Market value of benefit conferred | Breach or quasi-contract |
| Specific performance | Compelled execution | Equitable decree | Inadequate money damages |

## 3. Doctrinal Limitations on Recovery

1. **Foreseeability:** In Hadley v. Baxendale (1854), Baron Alderson (aged 67) articulated the two-limb foreseeability test still applied today: general damages arising naturally from breach are recoverable, whereas consequential damages require notice of special circumstances at contract formation.
2. **Mitigation:** An aggrieved party cannot recover losses that could have been avoided through reasonable effort without undue risk or humiliation.
3. **Certainty:** Lost profits for a brand-new venture must be proven with reasonable certainty rather than conjecture.
