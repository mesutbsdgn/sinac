# Forensic Medicine: Postmortem Toxicology and Redistribution

**Seminar:** Forensic Pathology & Legal Medicine — Module 6

## 1. Historical Perspective and Analytical Principles

Modern forensic toxicology traces its foundations to Mathieu Orfila, who died at age 65 after establishing chemical poison analysis in Parisian courts. Unlike clinical toxicology, postmortem interpretation must account for autolysis, bacterial neo-formation of ethanol, and passive diffusion of drugs from solid organs into central blood pools.

## 2. Specimen Selection and Redistribution Artifacts

Lipophilic basic drugs with large volumes of distribution—such as tricyclic antidepressants, digoxin, and methadone—migrate from myocardium, liver, and lungs into cardiac blood after death.

| Specimen Matrix | Primary Advantage | Major Limitation | Recommended Preservative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Peripheral (femoral) blood | Minimal redistribution artifact | Low volume in exsanguinated bodies | Sodium fluoride + potassium oxalate |
| Central (cardiac) blood | Generous sample volume | Artificially elevated concentrations | Sodium fluoride + potassium oxalate |
| Vitreous humor | Anatomically isolated, sterile | Delayed equilibrium for some drugs | Sodium fluoride |
| Gastric contents | Identifies recent oral ingestion | Does not prove systemic absorption | None (sealed container) |

Vitreous humor exhibits stable pH 7.0 postmortem longer than cardiac blood, and a minimum specimen volume of 5.0 mL is requested. A vitreous glucose reading of 216 mg/dL (12.0 mmol/L) supports antemortem hyperglycemia even after putrefaction begins.

## 3. Autopsy Case Review and Chain of Custody

Case discussion: Arthur Pendelton, a 52-year-old male (Case File #FT-2026-883), was found unresponsive with an empty amitriptyline bottle.

- Seal every tube with tamper-evident tape signed and dated across the lid margin.
- Record anatomical collection site explicitly; unlabeled "blood" is inadmissible in disputed overdoses.
- Store aliquots at 4 °C for routine screening and -20 °C for long-term confirmatory GC-MS or LC-MS/MS archiving.
