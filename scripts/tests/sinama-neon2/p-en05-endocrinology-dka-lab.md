# Adult Endocrinology: Diabetic Ketoacidosis Management and Lab Conversions

**Course:** Internal Medicine — Endocrinology & Metabolism Core Note

## 1. Diagnostic Triad and Fluid Resuscitation

Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) presents with hyperglycemia, ketonemia, and high-anion-gap metabolic acidosis. Restore intravascular volume first with balanced crystalloids or 0.9% NaCl at 15–20 mL/kg during the first hour before starting insulin.

- Verify serum potassium before initiating insulin; hold insulin and replete potassium if serum K+ is below 3.3 mmol/L.
- DKA resolution requires venous pH above 7.3 and serum bicarbonate at or above 15.0 mmol/L.

## 2. Regular Insulin Infusion Protocol

For a 70 kg adult without an initial bolus, continuous regular insulin at 0.1 units/kg/h equals 7 units/h. Aim for a steady plasma glucose decline of 50–75 mg/dL per hour while checking point-of-care glucose hourly.

When bedside glucose falls to 216 mg/dL (12.0 mmol/L), add 5% dextrose to maintenance fluids and lower insulin to 0.05 units/kg/h (3.5 units/h for a 70 kg patient). Maintain this combined regimen until the anion gap normalizes.

When nausea prevents oral intake after anion gap closure, administer **4.0 mg** IV ondansetron before transitioning to subcutaneous insulin.

## 3. International Metabolic Panel Reference

Because endocrine guidelines report both conventional US units and SI units, clinicians must convert lab values accurately:

| Analyte | Conventional Unit | SI Equivalent | Clinical Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Serum Creatinine | 1.5 mg/dL | 132.6 µmol/L | Monitor dehydration recovery |
| Total Cholesterol | 193.35 mg/dL | 5.0 mmol/L | Baseline lipid profile |
| Serum Triglycerides | 354 mg/dL | 5.2 mmol/L | Severe hypertriglyceridemia check |

## 4. Transition to Subcutaneous Insulin

1. Overlap subcutaneous basal insulin with the intravenous infusion for 1 to 2 hours to prevent rebound ketoacidosis.
2. Identify and treat precipitating triggers such as infection, missed doses, or SGLT2 inhibitor use.
3. Review sick-day ketone testing rules with the patient prior to discharge.
