# Clinical Psychology: Psychoeducation and Adherence in Bipolar I Disorder

**Course:** Adult Psychopathology & Evidence-Based Interventions — Week 10

## 1. Nosological Roots and Clinical Course

German psychiatrist Emil Kraepelin, at age 43 in 1899, distinguished manic-depressive insanity from dementia praecox in his sixth textbook edition. Contemporary bipolar I management combines pharmacotherapy led by psychiatry with structured psychological interventions that reduce relapse rates and hospitalization.

- **Social Rhythm Therapy (IPSRT):** Stabilizes circadian anchors such as wake time, first social contact, and mealtimes.
- **Relapse signature mapping:** Helps clients list idiosyncratic prodromal cues (decreased sleep need, pressured speech, increased spending).
- Cognitive-behavioral thеrapy modules emphasize sleep regularity and stimulus control during euthymic phases.

## 2. Collaborative Monitoring and Therapeutic Alliance

Non-adherence frequently peaks during early euthymia when patients miss hypomanic energy or experience subtle side effects. Psychologists coordinate with prescribers rather than adjusting medication independently.

| Assessment Domain | Structured Instrument | Clinical Trigger for Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manic symptom severity | Young Mania Rating Scale (YMRS) | Score trending upward across two visits |
| Depressive severity | MADRS or PHQ-9 | Active suicidal ideation or functional decline |
| Medication adherence | MARS-5 self-report | Missed doses > 20% over two weeks |

A Young Mania Rating Scale score above 12.0 points prompts urgent care-team communication. Client A. maintained stable mood logs while her psychiatrist kept trough serum lithium near 0.8 mmol/L and below the 1.2 mmol/L toxicity threshold (maintenance ceiling 1.0 mmol/L).

## 3. Intake Vignette for Seminar Discussion

Clinical vignette: Elena Vasquez (DOB 09/17/1988), a 38-year-old outpatient, reported three nights of reduced need for sleep before her intake session.

During formulation, examine sleep disruption both as an early symptom and as a causal driver that accelerates mood elevation. Involve trusted family members—with signed consent—to create a written crisis action plan while the client is euthymic.
