# Library Notice: Exam-Period Hours

From Monday 7 December the main library opens at 7:30 and closes at midnight on weekdays. Weekend hours are 9:00 to 22:00.

- Group study rooms on the second floor can be booked up to 48 hours ahead at the front desk or on the library website.
- Each booking lasts at most 2 hours, and rooms hold between 4 and 8 people.
- Please keep food and open drinks out of the quiet reading hall; closed water bottles are fine.
- The reading hall is kept at 21.0 °C, so bring a jacket if you feel cold.
- Lost property is held at the desk for 30 days.

Questions can be sent to the circulation team at the desk or by the usual library help form.
