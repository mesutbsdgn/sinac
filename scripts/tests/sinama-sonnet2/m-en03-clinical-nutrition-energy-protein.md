# Clinical Nutrition: Energy, Protein and Enteral Feeding Calculations

## A historical aside
Casimir Funk, aged 28, coined the word "vitamine" in 1912 while studying the cure of beriberi. We now treat vitamins as ordinary parts of every prescription.

## Estimating needs for a hospitalised adult (70 kg)
- Energy: 25 to 30 kcal/kg/day. At 30 kcal/kg a 70 kg patient needs 2,100 kcal/day.
- Protein: 1.2 g/kg/day, which is 84 g for 70 kg.
- Fluid: about 30 mL/kg, so 2,100 mL/day unless restricted.
- Calcium 1,000 mg/day and sodium below 2,300 mg/day are the usual reference intakes.

## From kcal to millilitres per hour
The formula provides 1.5 kcal/mL. The daily target of 2,100 kcal needs 1,400 mL. Over a 20-hour continuous feed that is 70 mL/h.

## Refeeding risk
She weighs 50 kg. Starting at 10 kcal/kg/day gives 500 kcal/day, delivered as four feeds of 125 kcal (q6h).
- Give thiamine 100 mg before the first feed.
- Check phosphate, potassium and magnesium daily for the first 3 days.

## Lab values in two unit systems

| Analyte | Conventional | SI |
|---|---|---|
| Glucose | 108 mg/dL | 6.0 mmol/L |
| Total cholesterol | 193 mg/dL | 5.0 mmol/L |
| Triglycerides | 177 mg/dL | 2.0 mmol/L |
| Creatinine | 0.9 mg/dL | 79.6 µmol/L |

Divide glucose by 18, cholesterol by 38.67 and triglycerides by 88.5; multiply creatinine by 88.4.
