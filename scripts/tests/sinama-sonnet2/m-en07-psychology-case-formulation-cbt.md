# Clinical Psychology Practicum: Writing a CBT Case Formulation

## The five-areas model
- Situation, thoughts, emotions, physical feelings, behaviour.
- The formulation links them and shows why the problem persists.

## Worked example
Client: Thomas Brennan (DOB 12 May 1991, file PSY-2026-0417).
Presenting problem: panic attacks on the commuter train, avoiding work meetings.
- Trigger: crowded carriage, heart racing.
- Thought: "I am about to collapse."
- Behaviour: gets off at the next stop; safety behaviour reinforces the fear.

Session 3 note: a 34-year-old teacher, Sarah Whitfield, reported that exposure homework had reduced her avoidance from daily to twice weekly.

## Why we anonymise
Notes shared with a class should use initials such as Mr. K. or labels such as Client 1, so a case can be discussed without exposing the person.

## Historical cases
- Phineas Gage was 25 when an iron rod passed through his frontal lobe in 1848, and his case founded modern neuropsychology.
- Freud's patient Anna O. is a pseudonym, which is the standard we follow.

## Measures and medication context
- Beck Depression Inventory scores of 0 to 13 are minimal, 14 to 19 mild, 20 to 28 moderate and 29 to 63 severe.
- Psychologists do not prescribe, but you should know common regimens: sertraline starts at 25 mg for a week, then 50 mg.
- Lithium requires a serum level of 0.6 to 1.0 mmol/L, checked 12 hours after the last dose.
