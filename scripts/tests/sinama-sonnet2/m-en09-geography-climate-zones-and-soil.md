# Physical Geography: Climate Zones, Altitude and Soil

## The Köppen groups
- A: tropical, every month averages at least 18 °C.
- B: arid, evaporation exceeds rainfall.
- C: temperate, mild winters.
- D: continental, cold winters and warm summers.
- E: polar, the warmest month stays below 10 °C.

## A scientist who changed the map
Alfred Wegener, 31, presented the idea of continental drift to the Geological Association in Frankfurt on 6 January 1912. Most geologists rejected it until seafloor spreading was documented in the 1960s.

## Altitude and temperature
Temperature falls about 6.5 °C per 1,000 m of climb. If the coast is 30 °C, then at 2,000 m the air is about 17 °C. At the summit of Kilimanjaro (5,895 m) the same rule predicts about -8 °C. Mount Everest rises to 8,848 m.

## Soil profile and acidity
- Topsoil (A horizon) is usually the darkest, about 5.0 cm thick in poor grassland and 30 cm in fertile prairie.
- A soil at pH 7.0 is neutral; pH below 5.5 limits many crops.
- Tropical soils are often leached and acidic; chalky soils are alkaline.

## Data practice
Station A records an average annual rainfall of 14 mm and a hottest month of 38 °C. Station B records 1,200 mm and a coldest month of 22 °C.

| Station | Rainfall (mm/year) | Coldest month | Likely group |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 14 | 12 °C | B (hot desert) |
| B | 1,200 | 22 °C | A (tropical) |

## Water quality note
Nitrate in groundwater above 50 mg/L is unsafe for infant feeding, so farmers near irrigated fields should test wells yearly.
